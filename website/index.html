Fly in squad colours, automatically

TBAS Sync keeps every TBAS livery on your PC up to date, so you see your squadmates the way they painted themselves.

  • Set it once, then forget itIt starts with Windows, sits in the tray and checks for new and updated skins every 10 minutes.
  • Only what changedIt downloads new skins and replaces updated ones, and leaves your own skins alone.
  • You’ll know when it’s doneA Windows notification tells you how many skins were installed.
Download TBAS Sync

Windows 10 or 11 with IL-2 Great Battles installed. Free and open source on GitHub.

Getting started

  1. Download and unzipExtract TBASSync.zip to a folder you’ll keep, for example Documents\TBAS Sync.
  2. Run TBASSync.exeIf Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  3. Confirm your IL-2 folderIt finds Steam installs automatically. Otherwise point it at the folder that contains bin\game\Il-2.exe.
  4. Let it syncThe first sync downloads the whole collection, around 1.7 GB. After that, only changes.

Skins not showing in game? Make sure custom skins are enabled in IL-2’s settings, then restart the game.

Skins are the work of their painters and are published on HSD. Previews shown without restricted symbols.
